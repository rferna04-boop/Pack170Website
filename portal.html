Health & Safety Required Annually

Annual Health & Medical Record (Med Forms)

Required for all youth and attending adults participating in Cub Scout activities. Parts A & B are required for regular pack meetings, day hikes, and overnight camping trips under 72 hours.

  • ✓ Parts A & B: Informed consent, release agreement & health history
  • ✓ Valid for 12 calendar months from the date completed
  • ✓ Fillable online PDF format with official instructions
